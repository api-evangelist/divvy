> **Acquired or rebranded.** This company was acquired, merged or renamed. Its API, if it still exists, is offered under another name. This profile is kept as a historical record and is not maintained or rated.

**Status:** acquired-by-bill (now bill spend & expense)

Divvy is a corporate card and spend-management platform (free software plus business credit cards) that was acquired by BILL and now operates as BILL Spend & Expense; getdivvy.com redirects to bill.com. Its API for budgets, users, virtual cards, transactions, and reimbursements is delivered through the BILL developer platform (developer.bill.com) as part of the BILL v3 API, authenticated with a Spend & Expense API token, with a documented webhook event surface, dated changelog, sandbox environment, and per-token rate limits. This profile was surfaced as an Insight Partners portfolio company and has been enriched from BILL's public developer surface.

**Areas:** Company · Payments · Spend Management · Corporate Cards · Expense Management · Fintech · Bills

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Divvy: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
